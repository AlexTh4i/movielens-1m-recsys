\begin{abstract}
This report investigates Popularity (IMDB Weighted Rating), Matrix Factorization (Biased SVD and Implicit ALS), and Neural Collaborative Filtering (GMF, MLP, Scratch NeuMF, Pre-trained NeuMF, and Explicit NeuMF) on MovieLens-1M (1,000,209 ratings, 6,040 users, 3,883 movies) under a leak-free per-user 80/10/10 temporal split and full-catalog evaluation. Biased SVD (MF) achieves the best explicit rating accuracy (Test RMSE = 0.8935), whereas NeuMF (Pre-trained) achieves superior Top-10 ranking (Test NDCG@10 = 0.0801, Recall@10 = 0.0775).
\end{abstract}
\section{Problem and Data}
MovieLens-1M contains 1,000,209 ratings across 6,040 users and 3,883 items (sparsity 95.74\%). See Figure~1 (`../results/figures/fig1_eda_distributions.png`).
\section{Related Work}
Covers Resnick et al. (1994), Koren et al. (2009), Hu et al. (2008), Rendle et al. (2009), He et al. (2017), and Ferrari Dacrema et al. (2019).
\section{Methods}
Formulates IMDB Weighted Rating, Biased Funk-SVD, Implicit ALS closed-form ridge updates, and dual-pathway GMF + MLP = Pre-trained NeuMF.
\section{Implementation}
Details vectorized tensor batching, binary-search negative sampling, and linear decomposition of the first MLP layer.
\section{Evaluation and Error Analysis}
See `../results/metrics.md`, `../results/error_analysis.md`, and Figures 3--8 in `../results/figures/`.
\section{Conclusions}
Supervision alignment (Implicit vs. Explicit) dominates architectural depth; pre-trained NeuMF and tuned Implicit ALS achieve Pareto-optimal accuracy and catalog coverage.
